A · Current, refined

Same idea as today: the head is a cut-out in the hexagon. Rounded corners so the hexagon matches the soft head. The face still takes its colour from whatever is behind it, so on a light page it turns into a white hole.

B · Visor

The head becomes a dark glass screen with softly lit eyes, mouth and antenna light. It reads as a device rather than a clip-art icon, looks the same on any background, and the antenna light works as a status light (it pulses while thinking). Head is 15% larger so the face holds up at small sizes.

C · Visor, eyes only

Same as B without the mouth. All emotion comes from the eyes, the antenna light and small head moves: the most restrained, and the "coolest", but sad and worried rely on eye shape alone.
